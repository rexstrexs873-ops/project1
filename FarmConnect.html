Sell at a fair price. Prove the condition. Pay for what arrives.

Produce leaves the farm in good shape and arrives bruised, so farmers get downgraded prices and buyers and farmers blame each other. FarmConnect records condition at pickup and at delivery, rates transporters on it, and shows every farmer if their price is fair.

83%of tomato farmers surveyed named damage during harvest or handling as their main loss cause.
34% vs 8%of farmers in Kaduna and Oyo reported losses from delayed sales, so market access is a northern problem.
<1%of tomatoes were lost outright on farms in the same sample. Most "loss" was downgraded produce.
Figures from an MSU policy brief on tomato losses in Nigeria. Treat as one study; validate with farmers you talk to.

Marketplace

Every listing shows a fair price meter. Order and choose a transporter by their handling record.

Farmer inbox

Answer offers as a farmer. Pick a farmer, then accept, counter or decline each offer. Sample buyers have already sent some offers, and they reply to your counters. To receive your own offers here, untick "Farmers reply automatically" in the Offers tab.

Streak rewards

Buy from the same farmer 3 times and your next order from them gets 20% off the crops. An order counts once its delivery is confirmed, and using the reward resets the streak. Amina Bello and Funke Adebayo start with sample progress so you can try it quickly.

Offers and negotiation

Make an offer on any listing. The farmer replies with a counter-offer based on the asking price, and you can accept, counter or walk away. The farmer’s replies are simulated for this demo. Once you agree, order at the negotiated price.

Live updatesLive

Updates from farmers, buyers and transporters as they happen. Background updates are simulated for the demo. Your own orders, pickups, deliveries and listings show up here too.

Route map

Pick a transporter and a buyer to see the road route between them, then open the directions in Google Maps. The map is hand-drawn from approximate coordinates, and distances and times are estimates. Google Maps shows the real road route.

● Transporter   ● Buyer   ━ Best route   ╌ Alternate   Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move. Approximate coordinates, not survey-accurate.
